CHALLENGE DESCRIPTION:
----------------------
In 1687, Queen Roselia offered Dr. Oldenburg a residency in her queendom's most
prestigious university on one condition: he continue his research on optics. He
found a diamond that "decomposes" light into 7 colors: truly a breakthrough.
She wore it proudly on her crown, the biggest stone among her crown jewels,
where in the rays of the sun that shone through the palace windows, illuminated
the court in a rainbow haze.

FILES PROVIDED:
---------------
- queen_roselias_diamond.zip
  - challenge_stego.tif

SOLUTION:
---------

1. INITIAL TRIAGE

   Extracted the zip and inspected the TIFF:
   - Size: 635 x 635
   - Sample format: 64-bit float grayscale
   - No obvious metadata hints

2. FIRST LAYER (DECOY QR)

   Rounding float pixels to nearest integer produces a clean black/white image.
   That image decodes as a QR and returns:
   - BITSCTF{blud_REALLY_thought_lmao}

   This looked intentional but was rejected by the platform (decoy flag).

3. SECOND LAYER (RESIDUAL ANALYSIS)

   Computed the residual image:
   - residual = original_float - round(original_float)

   Then normalized residual to visible range and exported `residual_norm.png`.
   On close inspection (very faint text), the hidden flag appears in tiny font.

4. REAL FLAG RECOVERY

   The residual layer reveals:
   - BITSCTF{qr_bu7_n07_7h47_qr}

5. VALIDATION

   Submitted the residual-derived flag and it is the accepted solve.

TOOLS USED:
-----------
- unzip / file / exiftool
- Python (tifffile, numpy, PIL/OpenCV)
- QR decoder (for identifying decoy layer)
- Manual visual inspection of `residual_norm.png`
