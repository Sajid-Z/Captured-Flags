4. DECODE STRATEGY
   
   Treat each symbol as 2-bit data:
   - U+200C -> 00
   - U+200D -> 01
   - U+202C -> 10

   - Concatenate into a bitstream
   - Convert to bytes
   - Decode bytes as UTF-16BE
